# Canada-U.S.-China ResearchGate V4

GitHub/Azure-ready policy intelligence workspace.

## V4 changes
- Domain-first retrieval; country names alone do not establish relevance
- Misleading-keyword rejection for recipes, restaurants, shopping, and personal questions
- Explicit policy domain, relationship, instrument type, record type, evidence status, and maintenance status
- Task-specific Compare, What changed, Brief, Gaps, Timeline, and Evidence outputs
- Missing comparison cells shown rather than filled with non-equivalent context
- Quota period end separated from instrument expiry
- Evidence-linked timeline with date types and significance
- Built-in retrieval regression suite under Quality & review
- Persistent local saved snapshots and editable briefing export
